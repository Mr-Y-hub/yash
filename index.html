~/whoami

Who I am and what I actually do.

My background is a mix of defensive and offensive work. Day to day at FIS that meant scanning, validating, prioritising and chasing remediation across a global estate — and writing the reports that went to people who had to make budget decisions off them.

The part I got good at was translation. Turning an unauthenticated RCE on an internet-facing appliance into something a non-technical director can act on is a different skill from finding it, and a lot of the job was exactly that.

Outside work it has meant hands-on offensive practice: penetration testing, web exploitation and reverse engineering. I write Python comfortably and have built and shipped small tools using LangChain and LLM APIs.

FIS Global · Remote
